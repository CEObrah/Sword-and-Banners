# Sword & Banners Narrative Direction Standard - 2026-09-02

## Target

The game should read as a persistent serialized historical/fantasy saga, not as a sequence of AI status summaries. The target qualities are earned buildup, distinct people, layered motives, material and political pressure, quiet connective scenes, reversals, consequences that carry forward, and meaningful aftermath. This is a craft target, not an instruction to imitate any named author's prose.

## Universal scene rule

The standard applies to **all narration**: family, travel, training, recovery, markets, councils, politics, investigations, downtime, personal combat, skirmishes, battles, sieges, aftermath, and transitions. Runtime command boundaries are invisible to the fiction unless their in-world consequence is itself perceptible.

Important scenes should be allowed to develop across turns. Internally use the sequence:

`approach / anticipation -> immediate objective -> friction -> development or reversal -> consequence -> aftermath / bridge`

Not every response contains every stage. Routine material compresses. Important material breathes.

## Combat and warfare

Combat is never `attack -> result -> status`, and warfare is never `order -> casualties -> next command`. The runtime owns every hard physical result. The LLM stages the committed chronology as a developing human scene with readable space, pressure, adaptation, wounds, fatigue, command delay, ally/enemy reaction, reversals, and aftermath. Repetitive exchanges may compress, but material turns must appear where they occur.

The GM may not invent a hit, wound, charge, movement, tactic, rout, surrender, death, reinforcement, or emotional fact merely to improve drama. Story quality comes from **how truthful consequences are staged and connected**, not from overriding them. See the Skill's `references/combat-and-warfare.md` plus `references/narration.md` and `references/scene-craft.md`.

## Long-form continuity

Scenes should leave residue when lawful state supports it. Injuries affect later movement and confidence. Public humiliation changes later interactions. Rescue creates obligation. Expense changes later political/economic choices. Family goodbyes color later reports. Victory can create overconfidence, jealousy, new duties, or logistical burdens only where current evidence and autonomous actor state support them.

Foreshadow through existing pressure and uncertainty, never through leaked GM-private truth. Do not manufacture cliffhangers every turn.

## QA rule

A narration draft fails if it mainly rephrases the previous response, converts runtime fields into prose, makes every NPC sound alike, skips the buildup and aftermath of a major event, or turns combat into telemetry. The Skill regressions now protect this doctrine.

For development defects, a confirmed P0/P1/systemic P2 found in one game requires inspection of the analogous subsystem in the other game before the issue is called globally fixed.
