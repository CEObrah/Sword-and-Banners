# House Tang, Sword Manor, and Personal Forces

House Tang is the large household, political-military institution, and force owner. Sword Manor is its training institution, martial academy, and headquarters complex inside Tang Manor. They belong to one institutional system but are not the same state owner.

House Tang's large guard, cavalry, disciple, institutional, and contracted military bodies remain unit/formation forces at appropriate mass resolution. They do not become individual-lite merely because Tang Wei can influence or command them.

Tang Wei's personally sworn force is cohort-first at scale. Ordinary retainers remain conserved provenance-backed cohorts even when Wei personally recruited them. Materialize a persistent person-lite or exact person only when an existing member becomes named, exceptional, socially important, specialized, command-relevant, wounded, promoted into an individually accountable office, or otherwise causally important. Materialization reclassifies that same body; it never creates additional personnel.

Troops issued to Tang Wei by Qin, another state, House Tang, a mercenary employer, or an allied commander retain their original unit/formation representation. An issued formation receives a command-assignment overlay; it does not become Tang Wei's personal force.

Sword Manor may maintain training and promotion standards for House Tang formations. House units can each have permanent role, doctrine, training plan, equipment standard, tendencies, cohesion, and command structure. Tang Wei's personal sub-units are named and organized only by the player or by explicitly delegated authority.
