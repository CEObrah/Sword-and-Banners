# House Tang Military Organization and Personal Forces

House Tang is a major household, political authority, economic owner, and military power. Its military hierarchy is recursive: zero-body command groups organize conserved troop formations without duplicating their manpower.

## One House Tang Grand Field Army

House Tang does not operate Inner Walls, the Four Bastion Corps, and the elite House troops as separate independent armies. They are permanent branches of one recursive House Tang Grand Field Army. Tang Zhu is its field commander and Tang Ling is its Chief Administrator. Both are command staff outside the fighting strength of every subordinate troop formation.

The permanent command tree is:

```text
House Tang Grand Field Army — Tang Zhu / Tang Ling
    -> Four Bastion Corps Command
        -> Iron Wall Corps
        -> Red Thunder Corps
        -> White Blade Corps
        -> Stone Spear Corps
    -> Inner Walls Field Command
        -> Senior Command
        -> General Command
        -> Junior Command
        -> Trainee Command
    -> House Guard Command
    -> Guardian Cavalry Command
    -> Tang Champions Command
    -> additional intact field armies or command groups when lawfully attached
```

A subordinate command is not a second army merely because it has its own commander and nested units. Zhu and Ling command the whole House force through the parent node. The branches retain their own officers, formations, specialties, history, readiness, equipment, and manpower ownership so that orders can be delegated without flattening the organization.

Additional complete command groups may be attached when lawfully ordered. An attached command remains intact as one recursive subtree. Its nested formations, subordinate commands, commanders, history, equipment, readiness, and manpower ownership are not flattened or duplicated.

## Tang Manor Defense in Depth

At home, House Tang deliberately does not expose its best troops to the first layer of routine siege attrition when a specialist force exists for that job.

The default defense is:

1. **Four Bastion Corps — outer perimeter.** Iron Rampart, Red Crane, White Lantern, and Deep Earth normally own the outer wall, gate, towers, moat, artillery positions, engineering works, breach response, and the first defensive contact.
2. **Inner Walls — city and interior belt.** Inner Walls normally protects the populated interior behind the outer perimeter, controls interior approaches, contains penetrations, and reinforces threatened sectors before an attacker can reach the Inner Citadel.
3. **House Guard / Guardian Cavalry / Tang Champions — Inner Citadel and decisive reserve.** These elite House branches normally protect the family seat and remain available as a high-quality mobile counterattack, sortie, rescue, pursuit, or decisive reserve.

These are doctrinal defaults, not artificial locks. Zhu, Ling when lawfully acting, or a properly delegated commander may send Bastion formations inward, Inner Walls outward, or elite House troops anywhere when breach geometry, casualties, fires, raids, field deployment, relief, pursuit, or another urgent operational need requires it. Quality preservation must never become an excuse to lose the estate, civilians, or a decisive battle.

The purpose is economy of force: use specialists where they are strongest, keep deeper layers fresh, and spend the highest-quality formations only when their capability has enough tactical or strategic value to justify the risk.

## Inner Walls

Inner Walls is a permanent House Tang troop-house and military force, not a public school, academy, civilian institution, or mobilization-only manpower pool. It does not admit or train outsiders as a commercial or public educational service.

Its current conserved fighting establishment is permanently organized under Inner Walls Field Command, led by its own commander, explicit staff, and successor order. Senior, General, Junior, and Trainee bodies remain internal military ranks and development cohorts of the Inner Walls force. Inner Walls may train and promote its own members through House Tang military standards, facilities, instructors, equipment, and verified elapsed training.

Inner Walls formations remain standing military formations. They do not disappear into a peacetime reserve merely because they are not campaigning.

## House Tang Troop Ownership

House Guard, Guardian Cavalry, Tang Champions, Inner Walls, and the Four Bastion Corps retain distinct conserved manpower owners and formations. This is accounting and organizational identity inside one army, not evidence that they are independent armies. Command attachment changes command relationships, not ownership. A formation or whole command attached to another field army remains the same bodies with the same provenance.

Top army, command-group, and maneuver-formation commanders and deputies are outside stated troop strength. Embedded 1,000-, 500-, and 100-man troop-echelon commanders are bodies inside the relevant formation strength.

A maneuver formation may contain multiple troop roles. Infantry, archers, crossbowmen, cavalry, engineers, or other lawful components can coexist inside one formation when that organization is designed as combined arms. The runtime must not collapse such a formation into a single-role blob during split, merge, casualty, reconstitution, or battle resolution.

## Tang Wei's Field Army

Tang Wei commands Tang Wei Field Army and any formations or command groups explicitly assigned to it. He is not First Sword and does not hold automatic command over the entire House Tang military establishment merely because he is House Tang's heir.

Tang Wei Field Army is itself a recursive command. When Zhu and Ling attach it to the House Tang Grand Field Army, the whole Tang Wei command becomes one additional subordinate command node while everything beneath it remains intact. The same principle applies if Tang Wei Field Army later joins a larger Qin or coalition army under lawful command arrangements.

Tang Wei's personally sworn force remains cohort-first at scale. Ordinary retainers stay conserved provenance-backed cohorts. Materialize a persistent person-lite or exact person only when an existing member becomes individually important; materialization reclassifies the same body and never creates another person.

State troops, allied troops, or House Tang troops temporarily assigned to Tang Wei retain their original manpower ownership. Command assignment never silently converts them into Tang Wei's personal retainers.
