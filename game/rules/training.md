# Training and Development

## Representation-neutral development
Unit representation is storage compression, never a training bonus. Exact, character-lite, and unit personnel use the same underlying development law. Training speed, promotion probability, experience, resource efficiency, and elite qualification cannot improve merely because a force is represented as one large object.

Development uses starting capability, aptitude, age/body state, scheduled hours, attendance, instructor quality and availability, student-to-instructor ratio, facilities, equipment, nutrition, health, fatigue, recovery, relevance, difficulty fit, real campaign experience, combat experience, current mastery, and diminishing returns.

## Capacity and experience
Instructor time, horses, ranges, armor, weapons, food, medical support, and facilities are finite. Basic mass drill can scale; elite development requires increasingly scarce correction, leadership opportunities, and real campaign experience.

## Promotion by transfer
Training changes capability. Promotion changes status. Units never jump wholesale from levy to elite. Qualified people transfer between quality populations and all headcount conserves.

## Elite resolution
As people become rarer, stronger, more specialized, higher-ranking, or politically important, resolve them in smaller units or as individual-lite/exact characters.

## Batch equivalence
Batch training must be materially equivalent to fine-grained settlement under unchanged conditions. Split batches at war, injury, promotion, equipment shortage, instructor change, assignment change, or other causal boundaries.
