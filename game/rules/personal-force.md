# Personal Force and Issued Detachments

A personal force is a persistent organization only when current state explicitly records it as such. Temporary operational control does not transfer underlying personnel ownership.

A **unit contains exactly one troop type**. Infantry, archers/crossbowmen, cavalry, chariots, engineers, medics, logistics troops, scouts, signal troops, and other materially distinct troop types remain separate units.

If Qin assigns Tang Wei 5,000 infantry and 2,000 archers, he may keep them as one 5,000-infantry unit plus one 2,000-archer unit, or split them further. If he halves both types, the correct result is four units: two 2,500-infantry units and two 1,000-archer units. He may then place one infantry unit and one archer unit under the same commander without merging them.

When an organization assigns an intact unit, its personnel remain owned by that source unit/force unless a lawful transfer changes ownership. Assignment changes command authority only.

Named officers remain separate persistent individuals. Ordinary unnamed personnel resolve through their unit aggregate. Materializing a named person deducts one real surviving member exactly once and preserves already-settled unit history.

A temporary issued detachment preserves source owner, home unit where applicable, troop type, headcount, equipment and horse custody, assignment, command relationship, start/end conditions, and return/transfer rules.

Returning an issued detachment restores surviving intact units to their home establishment and returns raw surviving personnel to the lawful source establishment. Return never resets casualties, injuries, experience, equipment/horse losses, cohesion, promotions, or history. The source owner then reconstitutes from real resources.
