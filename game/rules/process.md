# Causal world settlement

The world is represented by bounded causal hosts. A host sleeps until its next scheduled boundary, a material outward consequence reaches it, or a player action directly touches it.

Long time advances settle only hosts that are due. Recurring quiet intervals may be compacted arithmetically. Ordinary production paths never scan every person, faction, House, force, or formation to prove that nothing happened.

Autonomous actors use the same semantic commands and conservation rules as player actions. Routine choices are deterministic; strategic choices are scored from current goals, knowledge, resources, authority, threats, doctrine, and commitments. Hidden actions remain hidden until information is lawfully delivered.
