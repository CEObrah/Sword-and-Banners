# Independent Organizations, Mercenaries, and Non-State Forces

Independent institutions are persistent when they become materially relevant, but routine world activity remains aggregate. Do not create mandatory monthly micro-records for every school, guild, escort bureau, merchant association, mercenary company, raider group, clan, or local society. Material operations receive exact owners and clocks only when their state matters.

## Generic non-sovereign organizations

Schools, guilds, escort bureaus, trade associations, professional orders, clan associations, religious groups, and local societies that become materially persistent use the shared independent-organization owner. The organization records institutional identity, exact treasury, headquarters, policies, existing-person members/leaders, facilities/projects, and linked force refs while owning zero population itself.

Founding, funding, and withdrawal move real silver. Joining or appointing leadership never creates a body. An armed branch remains a normal conserved force/formation owner linked to the organization; the institution does not duplicate its troops. Physical projects use the same funded labor/material system as states and Houses. Organization-specific flavor remains data unless genuinely unique mechanics require a dedicated subsystem.

Persistent formations and Nested Armies remain their own military owners during travel and combat. An operation changes assignment or command authority, not ownership. Command groups own zero bodies and may organize intact subordinate Units without flattening them.

## Mercenary companies

A mercenary company owns its own conserved manpower, treasury, payroll, food, ammunition, equipment, mounts, contracts, losses, recruitment, training, headquarters/camp, and current employer as represented by its exact owners. A contract determines duty, deployment, payment, limits, and termination. It does not transfer manpower ownership unless a separate lawful transfer explicitly does so.

Mercenary availability may be projected through a market/index, but the projection owns no people. Companies do not respawn from a generic random table after defeat or after first contact. Audience-specific reputation state remains under `state/reputation/`.

## House Tang and fortress assignments

House Tang's permanent House Guard, Guardian Cavalry, Tang Champions, Four Bastion Corps, Inner Walls formations, and Tang Wei's separately owned personal forces are not mercenary bodies. Current fortress-defense assignments, contracted-company duties, House force roles, and retinue attachments are mutable exact state. Read them from current forces, command groups, formations, depots, orders, and contracts.

Fortified sites use their static exact defensive blueprint while cold. Strategically hot sites materialize finite military depots, installed fixed equipment, garrison support targets, magazines, civil/garrison grain, medical stores, repair capacity, water, wagon staging, damage, and siege-consumption state. Those military depots own stock only and never become demographic owners.

House Tang military stock is conserved separately from Qin state military stock. A House site does not implicitly draw Qin depot resources merely because Tang Manor lies inside Qin.

## Contract ledger

Every material contract records the exact parties and the terms that matter for execution, including payment, duration/renewal, duty, deployment authority, supplied food/housing/equipment where applicable, casualty obligations, surrender/termination authority, secrecy, legal limits, and command relationships. Loyalty changes only from supported causal evidence such as pay, treatment, losses, leadership, alternatives, family safety, known reputation, and survival expectations.

Equipment loaned by an employer remains the lender's property unless an explicit transfer says otherwise. A contract cannot grant proprietary mounts, breeding stock, production knowledge, or equipment ownership by implication.

## Mountain and steppe owners

Mountain populations, clans, pastoral confederations, raider networks, and fortified communities are persistent world owners when represented as current exact state rather than decorative prose. Named people remain separate exact identities. Anonymous warriors, hunters, scouts, skirmishers, craftspeople, and civilians remain aggregate unless individual relevance requires materialization.

Mixed civilians cannot enter combat without lawful manpower provenance, age/health eligibility, role, equipment, and authority. Raids and wars transfer or destroy real stock and create real casualties, displacement, resentment, and future scarcity. Defeated groups do not respawn simply because their template still exists.

Audience-specific reputation, renown, prestige, and notoriety live only under the reputation authority and update through delivered evidence; organization files do not own a universal reputation score.
