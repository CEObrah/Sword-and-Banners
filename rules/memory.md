# Memory, Context, Relationships, and Knowledge

`state/scene.json` is the hot resumption boundary. `state/index/owners.json` routes current typed owners. `state/mem/memory-episodes.json` stores durable episodic memory. `data/mechanics/memory.json` is the numerical authority for relationship-delta arithmetic.

## Authority layers

Current truth lives in typed state owners, not in summaries. The scene contains only what is required to resume the current interaction and parse the next action. Memory records preserve supported past events and holder-specific interpretations. Generated summaries and indexes are disposable projections and may never override their source owners.

Cold receipts, closed operations, old testimony, and detailed historical logs remain outside ordinary context unless a current dependency, investigation, audit, relationship, legal dispute, or explicit query requires them.

## Knowledge is holder-specific

World truth and character knowledge are separate. A fact becomes usable by a character only through a valid observation, message, document, testimony, report, inference, or other registered information path. Offscreen resolution does not automatically become Tang Wei's knowledge.

Rumor remains rumor. Repetition does not convert a claim into fact. Deception, uncertainty, contradictory testimony, and false beliefs remain attached to the holder and evidence that support them.

## Context compilation

At the start of a turn:

1. load `state/meta.json` and `state/scene.json`;
2. identify the active causal dependency graph;
3. use `state/index/owners.json` to retrieve only required exact owners;
4. load only rules and structured mechanics required by the declared action;
5. retrieve relevant relationship/knowledge/memory capsules only for active actors;
6. keep closed receipts and unrelated world registries cold.

A character, faction, army, institution, or project is not loaded merely because it exists.

## Character detail tiers

Unit representation stores distributions and aggregate history. Character-lite stores a permanent identity, body, capability, equipment, compact personality, relationships, current goal, and development ownership. Exact characters store the full persistent profile required by consequential play.

Increasing detail never creates a person, capability, relationship, item, memory, or history. Materialization conserves the source population and imports already-settled history exactly once.

## Personality and agency

Personality is persistent pressure, not mind control. Traits influence priorities, interpretation, risk tolerance, communication, and likely choices, but current evidence, relationships, orders, fear, injury, incentives, beliefs, and circumstances also matter.

No NPC may act from information they do not possess. Tang Wei's feelings and choices are never generated by relationship scores.

## Relationships

Relationships require causal evidence. Important relationships store the relevant dimensions such as trust, respect, affection, fear, resentment, duty, political alignment, or obligation. `data/mechanics/memory.json` resolves numerical deltas from saved events and attribution.

Relationship changes never occur merely because time passed. A time skip can settle relationship activity only when a valid recurring interaction, correspondence, shared duty, separation effect, conflict, or other supported process exists.

## Episodic and long-term memory

Preserve pivotal events, promises, betrayals, debts, appointments, major injuries, deaths, discoveries, relationship-defining incidents, legal precedents, secrets, and durable obligations. Routine conversation and repeated description may be compressed after their consequences are represented in current state and durable anchors.

Subjective memory requires evidence available to that person. Objective event history does not automatically define what a person remembers, believes, or feels about it.

## Offscreen people

Named people continue to develop only through their owned duties, training, travel, captivity, illness, projects, relationships, or other processes. Offscreen status grants no free advancement and no immunity from consequences. Development uses `rules/growth.md` and its structured mechanics exactly as it does for Tang Wei.

## Checkpoint and compression

A checkpoint may rebuild disposable indexes and summaries, compress routine closed history, and verify that current truth is represented by typed owners. It may not rewrite established facts for narrative convenience. If checkpoint settlement changes canonical state, it is a normal revision-locked transaction.

Validation records and cold forensic history are not ordinary gameplay context.

## Invariants

- one current fact has one authoritative owner;
- summaries never outrank source state;
- no duplicate person is created during materialization;
- no character receives knowledge without a valid path;
- no memory receives unsupported subjective interpretation;
- relationship changes require evidence;
- cold history stays cold unless causally relevant;
- context size is controlled by dependency retrieval, not by deleting world state.
