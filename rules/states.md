# Warring States World and State Macro

State macro values are campaign abstractions, not precise historical census claims. Monthly state reviews update only material strategic changes. Offscreen aggregate civil and military units do not receive exact individual awards until an identity or subformation materializes.




The world contains Qin, Zhao, Chu, Wei, Han, Yan, Qi, mountain communities, autonomous settlements, merchant networks, roads, rivers, fortresses, border regions, armies, households, schools, escort institutions, courts, and civilian populations.

The external world is unit-first. Seven state macro ledgers own population, military pools, treasury units, grain units, mounts, administrative capacity, stability, and mobilization readiness. These deterministic values are campaign simulation inputs, not claims of exact historical census totals.

Ordinary state soldiers remain military accounting pools. A real campaign activates exact homogeneous role units only after authority, command, objective, route, supply, equipment, finance, time, and opposition exist. Participating forces fight through their actual homogeneous units; inactive force pools remain noncombat accounting owners. Named commanders do not conjure armies.

State monthly processes settle finance, supply, recruitment, training, readiness, appointments, health exposure, routes, diplomacy, and active pressures. Weekly or daily reviews exist only for active operations, crises, movement, messages, or other exact clocks. Static source-identity names create no scheduler load. Current anonymous role incumbents settle through their institution/state process rather than private character clocks.

Current campaign date, player location, House legal status, property, contracts and territorial reach are mutable state and must be read from their current owners.




Seven state macro owners represent broad populations, military pools, treasuries, grain, mounts, administration, stability, and mobilization. The macro layer is the default offscreen resolution level.

## Materialization

A state force pool may materialize a campaign army only through a transaction that records:
- legal or political authority;
- commander and command chain;
- exact people removed from field, garrison, and reserve pools;
- equipment, mounts, ammunition, money, grain, fodder, wagons, and medical support;
- origin, route, movement rate, destination, mission, and withdrawal conditions;
- opposition and information basis;
- successor process and next due time.

A campaign army uses exact homogeneous units. When it disbands, surviving people and material return through exact transactions. Casualties, captures, desertion, promotions, and equipment losses remain conserved.

## Unit development

Equivalent units may share one calculation/receipt only while their activity, instruction, equipment, health, location, duty, and resources are equivalent. Any material difference splits exact unit settlement. A shared receipt records integer flows, minutes, credits, thresholds, injuries, promotions, equipment effects, and successor dates.

## Named identities inside units

A static source-canon name creates no body. When current evidence binds that identity to an aggregate source unit, population or role slot, materialization conserves exactly one real source person and imports supported accumulated development and health history once. An already materialized exact/lite person is never deducted again.

## Required state unit composition

Each state military owner stores mutually exclusive role, experience, age, and qualification distributions; officer depth; nonexclusive specialist counts; a unit-development composition profile; sparse mean EDU banks; and an exact monthly training program. Each state population owner stores mutually exclusive occupation and dependent counts plus exact occupational-development programs. A source identity cannot materialize until one real current source-person claim is resolved.

Monthly settlement records eligible people, planned and verified hours, duty and campaign losses, instructors, facilities, food, pay, equipment, health exposure, earned mean EDU, whole-person qualification flows, recruitment, casualties, demobilization, and successor keys. The state process may share a receipt across equivalent units, but any active army, shortage, epidemic, mutiny, command change, regional disruption, or different training program splits exact unit settlement.

## Universal training and campaign parity

Every military role uses complete nine-attribute and 35-skill unit sheets. Planned formal training hours by qualification are defined only in `data/mechanics/training.json`; actual verified hours still lose time to duty, campaign, instructor, facility, equipment, food, pay, health, and recovery constraints. Equal verified campaign exposure uses the same base EDU formula for the player, House Tang, Sword Manor, and every external state. Faction identity grants no experience multiplier.

## Units and accounting pools

A unit is one homogeneous troop type and can contain any lawful headcount. Combat resolves directly from that unit's current aggregate sheet, actual exposed frontage, equipment, morale, cohesion, commander relationship and orders. The resolver may calculate representative contacts internally but never creates or persists a second troop organization beneath the unit. Military pools are accounting owners and never fight directly.
