# World Arcs

Use this reference when campaign-scale political, military, institutional, or geopolitical pressure is material to play or OOC development.

`state/arc/kingdom-arcs.json` is the existing exact authority for active and dormant world arcs. Do not create a second plot or pressure owner.

Active arcs may receive recurring causal reviews without Tang Wei initiating them. Reviews choose an exact saved actor connected to the arc and one of that actor's saved goals or duties, then dispatch that actor into the registered owning domain. Exact characters and institutions may create bounded queued work. States, sovereign polities, and Houses may also spend conserved money to prepare a priority, but preparation/courier/staff expenditure remains `work_queued`: it does **not** prove the objective advanced and never increases arc momentum. Only an owning-domain result that establishes an independent material consequence may return `material_action_settled`. Factions may do so when their registered action actually consumes saved resources and changes a real owner/effect. `work_queued` and `intent_recorded` never increase arc momentum; blocked work can reduce pressure. The arc itself never rolls strategic success or writes another subsystem's result.

Dormant arcs do not activate merely because a historical date arrives. Their saved prerequisites must become true through campaign causality. An active arc with no lawful active driver and no saved objective does not progress by fiat.

Hidden arc activity remains hidden. A player-visible report requires a saved information path and actual propagation through an appropriate current location or channel. Every initiative owns an independent delivery route so one unreachable old report cannot block later reports. Undelivered reports are not player knowledge, but triggered unread reports remain discoverable through bounded hot-plus-archive continuation rather than disappearing when the event head rolls over. Once a one-shot report has durably settled, its scheduler host/event is garbage-collected immediately; durable report/history owners, not `state/runtime.json`, preserve the result. Informational report arrival never creates a pending wake by itself.

This layer is not a drama quota. It lets independent actors continue pursuing saved interests while Wei trains, travels, waits, or handles other business, so larger developments can emerge without requiring the player to initiate every causal chain.

A bounded autonomy working set is never permanent eligibility. If one review can score only part of a large exact formation set, use rotating or resumable routing so every lawful formation can eventually be considered.
