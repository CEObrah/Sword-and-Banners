# World Arcs

Use this reference when campaign-scale political, military, institutional, or geopolitical pressure is material to play or OOC development.

`state/arc/kingdom-arcs.json` is the existing exact authority for active and dormant world arcs. Do not create a second plot or pressure owner.

Active arcs may receive recurring causal reviews without Tang Wei initiating them. Reviews may choose an exact saved actor connected to the arc and one of that actor's saved goals or duties, then record an abstract initiative and pressure trend. Material consequences remain with their existing exact owners and reducers.

Dormant arcs do not activate merely because a historical date arrives. Their saved prerequisites must become true through campaign causality. An active arc with no lawful active driver and no saved objective does not progress by fiat.

Hidden arc activity remains hidden. A player-visible report requires a saved information path and actual propagation through an appropriate current location or channel. The runtime may retry a bounded delivery route; an undelivered report is not player knowledge.

This layer is not a drama quota. It lets independent actors continue pursuing saved interests while Wei trains, travels, waits, or handles other business, so larger developments can emerge without requiring the player to initiate every causal chain.

A bounded autonomy working set is never permanent eligibility. If one review can score only part of a large exact formation set, use rotating or resumable routing so every lawful formation can eventually be considered.
