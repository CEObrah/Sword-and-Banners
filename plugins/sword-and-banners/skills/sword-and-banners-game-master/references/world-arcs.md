# World Arcs

Use this reference when campaign-scale political, military, institutional, or geopolitical pressure is material to play or OOC development.

`state/arc/kingdom-arcs.json` is the existing exact authority for active and dormant world arcs. Do not create a second plot or pressure owner.

Active arcs may receive recurring causal reviews without Tang Wei initiating them. Reviews may choose an exact saved actor connected to the arc and one of that actor's saved goals or duties, then record an abstract initiative and pressure trend. Material consequences remain with their existing exact owners and reducers.

Dormant arcs do not activate merely because a historical date arrives. Their saved prerequisites must become true through campaign causality. An active arc with no lawful active driver and no saved objective does not progress by fiat.

Hidden arc activity remains hidden. A player-visible report requires a saved information path and actual propagation through an appropriate current location or channel. The runtime may retry a bounded delivery route; an undelivered report is not player knowledge.

This layer is not a drama quota. It lets independent actors continue pursuing saved interests while Wei trains, travels, waits, or handles other business, so larger developments can emerge without requiring the player to initiate every causal chain.

A bounded autonomy working set is never permanent eligibility. If one review can score only part of a large exact formation set, use rotating or resumable routing so every lawful formation can eventually be considered.

## Convert lawful pressure into play

Background progression is not player-facing gameplay until a lawful consequence actually reaches Tang Wei. When fresh context exposes `opportunities`, treat them as non-authoritative routing cues built from already-triggered player-visible reports, messages, institutional responses, and other delivered developments. Inspect the exact `interaction_ref` before a consequential response when details materially matter.

Do not generate a random hook merely because play is quiet, and do not bury a delivered report merely because it originated offscreen. The desired chain is `saved actor or institution -> lawful action -> committed consequence -> information/contact route -> player-visible opportunity -> scene or decision`.

When fresh context advertises `advance_until_event`, use it for a standing interval intent such as `wait until something happens`, `continue until summoned`, or equivalent. It is a surface intent over the authoritative time-advancement path, not a second clock or scheduler. The caller may provide a bounded horizon; when none is provided, use the runtime-advertised default. Preview remains hidden-future. Execute may commit before that horizon when the causal runtime reaches a protected or player-facing wake; otherwise it reaches the requested horizon. Refresh context after every commit and stop for a protected wake, player-facing opportunity, delivered report, material decision, or resource problem. If the horizon is quiet and the already-declared standing intent still applies, continue only with a fresh revision and a fresh request ID. Never predict or probe the next hidden scheduler boundary merely to decide how far to advance.

`scene_vitality` is presentation permission, not campaign authority. It may support reversible local motion, routine background work, brief greetings, and spatially plausible entry or exit by a nearby established person. It may never create knowledge, relationships, promises, resources, injuries, offices, persistent movement, mission state, named staffing, or any later callback that depends on saved truth.
